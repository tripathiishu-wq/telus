How TELUS got here

the arc you'd inherit
1990
Alberta Government Telephones privatised

Roots as a provincial Crown telco — origin of the TELUS name and Western Canada stronghold.

1999
BC TELECOM merger

Merges with BC Tel, cementing dominance across BC and Alberta — base of today's fibre footprint.

2000s
National wireless expansion

Builds coast-to-coast mobile, joins the entrenched "Big Three."

2013
Verizon threat evaporates

Verizon rules out entering Canada — no serious foreign competitor has arrived since.

2020
TELUS International (Digital) IPO

The BPO/AI-services arm goes public — diversification beyond connectivity begins in earnest.

2023
5G Standalone network live

Lower latency, network slicing, IoT readiness — the platform for the next B2B revenue layer.

2025
Infra-monetisation & Digital taken private

Sells stakes to deleverage; buys back 100% of TELUS Digital (~US$539M) to own the AI/SaaS engine.
