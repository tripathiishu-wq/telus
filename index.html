04

How TELUS got here

the arc you'd inherit

1990

Alberta Government Telephones privatised

Roots as a provincial Crown telco — origin of the TELUS name and Western Canada stronghold.

1999

BC TELECOM merger

Merges with BC Tel, cementing dominance across BC and Alberta — base of today's fibre footprint.

2000s

National wireless expansion

Builds coast-to-coast mobile, joins the entrenched "Big Three."

2013

Verizon threat evaporates

Verizon rules out entering Canada — no serious foreign competitor has arrived since.

2020

TELUS International (Digital) IPO

The BPO/AI-services arm goes public — diversification beyond connectivity begins in earnest.

2023

5G Standalone network live

Lower latency, network slicing, IoT readiness — the platform for the next B2B revenue layer.

2025

Infra-monetisation & Digital taken private

Sells stakes to deleverage; buys back 100% of TELUS Digital (~US$539M) to own the AI/SaaS engine.

Your advantages & your soft floor

the CEO thesis

MOAT

Best-in-class churn

0.91% postpaid churn — lowest of the Big Three. Loyalty and bundling discipline you protect at all costs.

MOAT

Diversification beyond pipes

Health, Agriculture, Digital give revenue lines rivals can't copy — margin when telecom is flat.

MOAT

Fibre + 5G SA footprint

Deep FTTH in the West plus a live 5G Standalone core = premium internet ARPU and B2B/IoT optionality.

RISK

Mobile ARPU erosion

Competitive pricing drags phone ARPU. Decelerating — but the number the Street watches.

RISK

Saturation ceiling

92% internet, ~88% smartphone. Growth must come from ARPU, adjacencies and share-steal.

RISK

Capital intensity & debt

Spectrum and fibre pushed sector leverage above norms — hence infra stake sales.

◈ The 90-second board pitch: "TELUS wins by being the loyalty leader in a saturated market — I defend the 0.91% churn, accelerate the health/digital/IoT flywheel to escape the ARPU trap, and monetise passive infrastructure to fund the fibre endgame without diluting shareholders. We don't out-scale Rogers; we out-retain and out-diversify them."

The flanker-brand war

each Big Three carrier runs a fighter brand

Below the flagship, each incumbent runs mid-tier and prepaid brands on the same towers — identical coverage, separate service — to absorb price-shoppers without dragging premium ARPU down. This is where Freedom's low-cost 5G is attacked. Fido is Rogers' flanker; Koodo is yours.
